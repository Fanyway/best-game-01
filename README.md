LUMEN DRIFT — pilot a light orb through a dark arena where **light is a weapon**. Your lamp drains as the dark closes in; shards feed it back. Bank shards to widen your light — or spend 8✦ on a **PRISM (Q)** that bends light into a beam that burns sentinels. Prisms placed close together **LINK** into a chain of light that burns anything it touches; linked prisms burn 25% harder, and chain kills pay 25% extra — cluster them into a web, or spread them for range. Every third wave the sun dies: an **ECLIPSE**. Kills pay double and prisms link farther — but **WRAITHS** rise, pale ghosts that hide in your light. A beam cannot burn a wraith inside your light: lure it into the dark, or spend bank to shrink your lamp and expose it. Patrollers drift, chasers hunt, splitters burst into minis, and a boss waits on wave 10. Clear all ten waves to win. A complete 2D canvas arcade game: title, HUD (score, combo, light, bank, links, lives, wave), power-ups, pause, win and game-over screens, synthesized sound with an ambient drone, shimmer and sub-bass, and restart.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000/game/ in a desktop browser.
